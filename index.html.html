About

I'm a Level 1 SOC Analyst in Abu Dhabi. My day is real-time monitoring, alert triage and incident investigation: checking whether an alert is real, working out how far it reaches, and writing it up so someone can act on it.

My background is forensic science (B.Sc. and M.Sc.), and it shapes how I work. I correlate logs across endpoints, network and user activity, and I document findings the way a case file should read: clear, sourced and reproducible. I'm certified in Ethical Hacking (CEH) and Advanced Penetration Testing, and I keep building skills through hands-on labs and CTF-style challenges.

Research

Prediction of Criminal Tendencies by Social Media Usage and Personality Characteristics

Int. J. Adv. Res. 12(10), 1653-1671, October 2024. Satheesh, Prem, K.P., Sahay

Survey of 105 young adults on Big Five traits, social media use and criminal tendencies. Personality traits, especially conscientiousness, openness and emotional stability, explained more of the variance in criminal tendencies than social media use did.

Exploring the Prevalence of Psychological Autopsy in Indian Context

Indian Journal of Forensic Medicine and Toxicology 18(2), April-June 2024. Prem, Satheesh, K.P., Sahay

Questionnaire study of 50 mental health professionals, forensic psychologists and academics. 84% knew the term, but 72% had never been involved in a case, and respondents called for standardized protocols and training.

Forensic Identification of Charred Human Remains Using Dental Remnants (400-800°C)

Medico Legal Update 24(3), July-September 2024. Prem, Satheesh, K.P., Bansal

Review of over 50 sources on how heat changes enamel, dentine, pulp and dental restorations, and how dental records can still support identification of burned remains.
